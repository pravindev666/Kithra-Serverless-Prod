## 2. EXPERIMENT 1: The Mom Test Discovery Interview Series
* **What We Need to Learn:** Do urban Indian singles experience acute, actionable pain from superficial matchmaking, and what have they actually done to solve it?
* **Who Participates:** 25 non-affiliated Indian working professionals (Ages 22–36; Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, NCR) recruited using the neutral invitation script.
* **What We Measure:**
  - % of participants who describe a specific past relationship failure caused by values/communication mismatch.
  - % of participants who have spent money or significant time on active workarounds in the last 12 months.
* **Pre-Agreed Falsification Criteria:**
  - **FALSIFIED IF:** Fewer than 10 out of 25 participants can recall a specific past event where psychological incompatibility was the primary dealbreaker.
  - **ACTION IF FALSIFIED:** Stop marketing Kithra as a "deep psychometric solution." Pivot messaging to lifestyle alignment or social activity matching.

## 3. EXPERIMENT 2: The 5-Question Mobile Usability Test
* **What We Need to Learn:** Can a new user complete the 5-item starter battery on their own smartphone without coaching, confusion, or drop-off?
* **Who Participates:** 10 diverse adults (5 men, 5 women; varied tech comfort) observed over Google Meet screen share or in-person.
* **What We Measure:**
  - Task completion rate for Questions 1–5.
  - Median time to complete the 5 questions.
  - Number of clarifying questions asked by the participant.
  - Drop-off / hesitation points.
* **Pre-Agreed Falsification Criteria:**
  - **FALSIFIED IF:** More than 3 out of 10 users abandon before Question 5, OR if median completion time exceeds 3.5 minutes.
  - **ACTION IF FALSIFIED:** Cut the starter battery to **3 single-sentence questions**; eliminate complex scenario text.

## 4. EXPERIMENT 3: The Blind Choice Test (Psychology vs. Photo/Status)
* **What We Need to Learn:** Will users choose to connect with someone because of high psychological alignment, or do physical photos and college/employer pedigree dominate choices?
* **Who Participates:** 20 active dating/matrimony seekers.
* **What We Do:** Show each participant 3 blind profiles:
  - Profile A: High psychological compatibility (92/100), deep communication alignment, but minimal photo.
  - Profile B: Standard dating profile with an attractive photo and generic bio ("I love travel, food, and dogs").
  - Profile C: High-status matrimonial profile (Top tier college, high income, conventional family background).
* **What We Measure:** Which profile the participant chooses to unlock or message first, and their unprompted verbal reasoning.
* **Pre-Agreed Falsification Criteria:**
  - **FALSIFIED IF:** Fewer than 5 out of 20 participants select Profile A first.
  - **ACTION IF FALSIFIED:** Accept that physical attraction and social signaling are necessary gatekeepers. Redesign Kithra to combine high-quality photography *alongside* psychology, rather than de-emphasizing photos.

## 5. EXPERIMENT 4: The 5-Pair Manual Concierge Introduction
* **What We Need to Learn:** Does matching two people based on Kithra's psychological vectors lead to an unhurried, warm conversation in the real world?
* **Who Participates:** 10 consenting pilot participants (forming 5 pairs) whose questionnaire vectors show high alignment or healthy complementarity.
* **What We Do:** The founder manually introduces each pair via WhatsApp or Email with a shared context note (e.g. *"Both of you value quiet evening chats and take time to recharge after big workweeks"*). We do not use the automated web chat (since chat is currently mock state).
* **What We Measure:**
  - % of pairs where both members respond to the introduction within 24 hours.
  - Number of messages exchanged over 72 hours.
  - Voluntary post-introduction feedback: *"Did this introduction feel more relevant and comfortable than a typical app match?"*
* **Pre-Agreed Falsification Criteria:**
  - **FALSIFIED IF:** 3 or more pairs fail to exchange even 3 messages, OR if participants report feeling awkward and having nothing to talk about.
  - **ACTION IF FALSIFIED:** The matching algorithm is producing theoretical compatibility without conversational chemistry. Introduce guided conversation icebreakers or joint activity prompts.
