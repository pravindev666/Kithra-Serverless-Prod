Making genuine new friends as an adult is hard. For most of us, building meaningful new connections isn’t easy. Not only are there fewer opportunities to meet new people, but we become busier and pickier as we grow older. It also can take lots of time to get to know someone and it’s never certain that a deep friendship will come out of it. That’s why many of us throw in the towel and simply stop trying. We3 was built to solve this problem in a fast and easy way, but without losing sight of the ultimate goal: to form meaningful human connections. It all starts with you. Tell us about yourself. We’ll do the rest. You are unique, complex and multifaceted. This means that to connect you to the most compatible people around you, we need to really understand what makes you tick. What inspires you? What activities do you enjoy doing? What topics keep your mind interested? What role does religion play in your life? What makes you lose your cool? On their own, these questions might not say much about you. But put them together, along with many others, and it’s the story of what you were, who you are, and who you want to be. With this complete picture—and with help from social science and machine learning—we are able to understand and predict the profile of the people you will feel an instant connection with.
