No one should feel like the odd one out. The goal is interesting matches, not perfect ones. How do I book? Sign up, answer a few questions about yourself, then pick your city and day. We'll handle the rest. We'll create your group, book the restaurant, and let you know the time and place. Who can use Timeleft? Anyone 18+ who's looking to form new friendships. We're in 200+ cities across 52 countries, and we're building a space where everyone belongs, regardless of age, gender, orientation, or background.
