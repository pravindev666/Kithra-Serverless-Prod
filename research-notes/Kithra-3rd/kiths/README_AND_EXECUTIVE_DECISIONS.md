## 2. Executive decisions (BLUF)

1. **Reality.** Kithra is a deterministic, explainable, three-mode human
   connection platform. **No AI twins, no LLM matching, no inference billing.**
2. **Old brand rejected.** Midnight-indigo/iris is removed. **Recommended
   direction: D2 "Culture-Neutral Editorial" (Ink & Clay)** — teal + clay,
   mode-neutral, all text roles clear AA in light and dark. Alternatives D5 and
   D1 documented. **Founder sign-off required.**
3. **Pricing.** Three mode-gated subscriptions are the "big cat / small cat"
   error and are removed. **Recommendation: free core + unified Plus/Connect +
   real-cost add-ons.** Safety, discovery and post-consent chat are always free.
4. **Type.** Fraunces (display) + Inter (body) + Noto Sans per-script for Indic.
5. **Landing.** Three hero narratives tested; **ship "your kind of people"**
   first, keep the three-journeys variant for SEO.
6. **Admin.** Separate, deny-by-default console with MFA, RBAC, JIT case-scoped
   evidence and hash-chained audit. No raw questionnaires to moderators.
7. **Motion.** `motion/react`; strict budgets; reduced-motion mandatory.
8. **Honesty.** No E2EE claim, no guaranteed outcome, no fabricated proof.
