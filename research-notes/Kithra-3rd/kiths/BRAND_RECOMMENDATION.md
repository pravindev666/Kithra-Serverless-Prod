# BRAND RECOMMENDATION — with founder sign-off gate

## Recommendation

**Primary direction: D2 — Culture-Neutral Editorial ("Ink & Clay").**
Weighted score **4.85 / 5.00** (see `PALETTE_SCORECARD.csv`, computed).

- **Canvas** `#FBFAF7` · **Surface** `#FFFFFF` · **Text** `#1F2320` (15.25:1) ·
  **Muted** `#5A6159` (6.12:1)
- **Primary** `#1F6F5C` deep teal (6.02:1 white-on) · **Accent** `#D98A3D` clay
  (decoration; use `#8A5A1E` for text) · **Support** `#3E4A6B` ink-blue (8.76:1)
- **Type:** Fraunces (display) + Inter (body), Noto Sans superfamily for Indic.
- **Mode accents:** Friendship teal `#1F6F5C`, Dating rose-clay `#A83E50`,
  Matrimony ink-blue `#3E4A6B` — always icon + label, never colour alone.

**Alternatives:**
- **Alt-1: D5 Sophisticated Nature (Forest & Sand)** — 4.70; best raw contrast
  (7.64), sincere and calm; slightly "environmental" coding.
- **Alt-2: D1 Sunlit Humanist** — 3.81; warmest, closest to the founder's
  Sunlit instinct, but rose-clay skews romance-coded.

## Why D2 (reasoned, not trendy)

1. **Spans all three modes.** Teal + clay is neither pink (dating cliché) nor
   bridal (matrimony cliché) nor corporate indigo (rejected). It is the only
   direction that scores ≥4 across warmth *and* mode-neutrality.
2. **Measured accessibility.** Every text role clears AA by a wide margin; the
   clay accent is deliberately restricted to decoration after measurement.
3. **Culturally neutral.** Works for Indian and international audiences without
   asserting a cultural aesthetic; warmth comes from *material and photography*,
   not from colour stereotyping.
4. **Admin-separable.** The ink-blue support hue gives the admin console its own
   calm density without inventing a second brand.
5. **Evidence-aligned.** Echoes the verified strengths of the strongest
   competitor brand (Hinge: near-neutral base + a single non-pink accent +
   serif/sans pairing) without copying any of its assets.

## Assumptions behind the recommendation

- That warmth in Kithra should come primarily from **photography and motion**,
  not from hue (testable — hero comprehension tests will check this).
- That a neutral base maximises the *meaning* of the three mode accents.
- That Indian users do not require a culturally "Indian" palette to feel
  included (must be tested in-market — do not assert).
