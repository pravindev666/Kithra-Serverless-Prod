Readable companion to `EVIDENCE_REGISTER.csv`. Each entry: what it is, what it
actually supports, and **what it does not**. Tiers: **T1** peer-reviewed /
standard / regulator / official operator source · **T2** industry guidance ·

## Peer-reviewed & standards (T1)

**van der Zanden, Mos, Schouten & Krahmer (2022).** *What People Look at in
Multimodal Online Dating Profiles.* Communication Research.
`https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/0093650221995316`
→ Pictures and text are processed differently and **both** shape first
impressions. **Supports:** photo + specific text on cards; not photo-only.
**Does not support:** any claim about a specific layout converting better.

**McGloin & Denes (2018).** *Too hot to trust.* New Media & Society.
`https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1461444816675440`
→ Enhanced photos can lower perceived trustworthiness. **Supports:** honest
photo guidance; avoid glamour retouching of "typical members".
**Does not support:** universal effect sizes across cultures.

**Beauty-filter study (2023).** *Computers in Human Behavior.*
`https://www.mcm.uni-wuerzburg.de/fileadmin/06110000/2026/1-s2.0-S0747563223002224-main.pdf`
→ Filters raised attractiveness but reduced trust (male profiles, women raters).
**Supports:** discourage heavy filters. **Limitation:** single population.

**Survey-fatigue systematic review (2025).** PMC11833437.
`https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11833437/`
→ Fatigue causes careless responses and reduced responsiveness. **Supports:**
batch items, save/resume, coverage gating instead of a 100% demand.
**Does not support:** a specific "items before fatigue" number for Kithra.

**Long-survey fatigue (2022).** *Journal of Development Economics.*
`https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304387822001341`
→ Measurable within-survey deterioration. **Limitation:** field-survey context.

**WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum).**
`https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html`
→ ≥24×24 CSS px at AA. **Note:** the W3C URL returned **403** to our capture;
the criterion is cited from accessible mirrors and the normative spec.
**Kithra exceeds it:** ≥44 px effective targets.

**FTC (2022).** *Bringing Dark Patterns to Light.*
`https://www.ftc.gov/reports/bringing-dark-patterns-light`
→ documents fake urgency, hidden costs, difficult cancellation, forced consent.
**Supports:** one-step cancel, no countdown, unchecked consent.

## What the literature collectively does NOT support

- ❌ Any specific palette improving conversion (no causal evidence found).
- ❌ Deep questionnaires guaranteeing better relationship outcomes for Kithra.
- ❌ A 100% score meaning a happy marriage.
- ❌ AI twins replicating a mind.
- ❌ Inventing retention or accuracy statistics.
