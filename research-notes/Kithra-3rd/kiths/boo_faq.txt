All the basic features on Boo are completely free: Love, Pass, and messaging with matches. What is the minimum age requirement for Boo? The minimum age requirement for Boo is 18 years old. If you are not yet 18, you can join and start using Boo once you reach this age. What are personality types? At Boo, our algorithms are driven primarily by personality frameworks, ours specifically borrowing from the Jungian psychology and the Big Five (OCEAN) model. We use personality types to help you understand yourselves and each other—your values, strengths and weaknesses, and ways of perceiving the world. You can read more about why we use personality types . Personality Matching What is the MBTI (Myers Briggs)? MBTI is a personality framework that categorizes all people into 16 personality types. It provides a theory for how personality is derived as a function of how we perceive the world differently. It is based on the work of Swiss psychiatrist, Carl Jung, the father of analytical psychology. What are the 16 Personality Types? You can find all the personality types here . You may also select specific personality types in the Filter on the app. Boo Account How do I create an account on Boo? You can create an account on Boo by downloading our free app from the Apple App Store for iOS users or from the Google Play Store for Android users. How do I restore my account or log in from a different device? To restore your account or log in from a different device, enter the email address you used during the registration process. Is there a Boo app for PC? There is currently no Boo app download for PC, but you can access the Boo website through your internet browser. The web address for Boo is boo.world . How can I revisit the tutorial? You can revisit the tutorial by navigating to Settings and selecting the "View Tutorial" option. This will reset the tutorial, so that the tips appear as you navigate the app. How do I manage app notifications? You can manage your app notifications by going to Settings and tapping on "Notifications". Why am I not receiving push notifications? Ensure that push notifications are enabled for Boo in the app’s Settings (Settings > Notifications) and your phone's settings. If the problem persists, contact us at hello@boo.world . Is there a "dark mode" option? Yes, you can enable "dark mode" by locating the option in the Settings menu (Settings > Appearance and Display > Dark Mode). How can I log out of my account? To log out of your account, go to Settings, select "My Account", and then tap "Logout". Boo Profile How do I edit my profile? To edit your profile, navigate to your profile and select "Edit” at the top right of the screen. Where can I change my name or Boo ID? You can change your name or Boo ID in the "Edit Profile" section. Simply tap on the respective field that you wish to update. How do I change my birthday or correct my age? We do not currently offer the option to directly change your age or birthday in the app. To change your birthday, you will need to contact our support team through the app's Settings under "Send Feedback", or by emailing us at hello@boo.world with your Boo ID. How do I remove my height from my profile? Scroll to the top until nothing is selected, then press the “Continue” button. How can I adjust my preferences for who I'm "Looking For"? In the "Edit Profile" section, you will find the "Looking For" field, which you can adjust according to your preferences. How do I delete or manage my photos? You can manage your photos in the "Edit Profile" section. To delete a photo, tap the "x" icon on the top right corner of the photo. Please note that you are required to have at least one photo on your account. How do I change my profile picture? Go to "Edit Profile" and upload your picture with the plus symbol. How do I add an audio recording to my profile? Go to "Edit Profile" and "About Me", then click the microphone icon at the bottom left. Can I add videos to my profile? Absolutely! You can add a video of up to 15 seconds in length to your profile. Simply upload it the same way as you would a photo, in the “Edit Profile” section of the app. How can I retake the personality quiz? If you wish to retake the personality quiz, go to your account page, select the "Edit" option below your profile picture, then tap on "16 Type" followed by "Retake Quiz".
