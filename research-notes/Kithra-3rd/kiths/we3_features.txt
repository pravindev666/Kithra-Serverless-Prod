Making genuine new friends as an adult is hard. Not only are there fewer opportunities to meet new people, but we become busier and pickier as we grow older. For most of us, building meaningful new connections takes lots of time and it’s never certain that something will come out of it. That’s why many of us throw in the towel and simply stop trying. We3 was built to solve this problem in a fast and easy way, but without losing sight of the ultimate goal: to form lasting and profound human connections. Not for love. Make connections, plans and memories, without making moves. Instead of matching 2 users who “swiped right” on each other’s profiles, We3 matches you in groups of 3 highly compatible users. There is an endless graveyard of apps that focused on friendship, but were hijacked by members with romantic intentions. The ones that remain alive today, are largely dating apps still masquerading as friendships apps. By always connecting you in groups of three, we are ensuring we won’t go down that path. Private profiles. Your We3 profile is never public. Only your Tribes can see you. We respect your privacy. That’s why your profile is never public. The only people that will be able to see your profile photo are those in your Tribes. Your answers on We3 are also safe. No one—not even the people in your Tribe—are able to see how you responded to individual questions or even certain topics. Smart Matching Using science to make the best connections possible. We3 tries hard to connect you to the people that you are most likely to get along with. We take into account decades of research into the psychology and sociology of friendship and compatibility. However, despite the contribution social science has made on our understanding, it has been unable to conduct controlled experiments that allow us to isolate individual factors and their contribution to friendship quality. We3 mixes social science with computer science, by using an algorithm that considers over 150 individual characteristics when deciding who to connect. And thanks to machine learning, we are able to continually improve the predictive validity of our algorithm’s decisions. Two is a date. Three is a party. Get started with We3.
