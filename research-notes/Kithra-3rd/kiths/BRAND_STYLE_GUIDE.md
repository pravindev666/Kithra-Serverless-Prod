# BRAND STYLE GUIDE — DO / DON'T & VOICE

Applies to every surface: landing, member app, admin, email, notifications,
app-store copy, support macros.

---

## 1. Voice and tone

**Personality:** warm, intelligent, plain-spoken, never moralising, never
manipulative. Kithra sounds like a thoughtful friend who respects your privacy
and your time — not a fortune teller, not a government form, not a hype machine.

| Principle | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Honesty | "agreement across the answers compared" | "100% soulmate", "destined" |
| Warmth | "Nice to meet you." | "Onboarding step 1 of 4" |
| Respect | "You can change this any time." | "You must complete this to continue." |
| Clarity | "This takes about 12 minutes." | "Just a few quick questions!" |
| Privacy | "Private by default." | "We'll never share your data. Ever." |
| Safety | "Blocking is always free." | "Premium members are safer." |
| Limits | "Questionnaires can't read minds." | "Our science knows you." |

**Tone shifts by context**
- **Landing:** confident, editorial, inviting.
- **Quiz:** calm, neutral, unhurried; no streak pressure, no timers.
- **Empty states:** matter-of-fact, forward-looking; never fake candidates.
- **Errors:** plain, specific, actionable; never blame the user.
- **Safety:** direct, unembellished, no marketing adjectives.
- **Admin:** terse, factual, no brand flourish.

## 2. Visual DO

- Use the neutral canvas so the **three mode accents mean something**.
- Use **≥44 px** targets and a visible focus ring everywhere.
- Pair **one photograph** with **one specific line of text** on cards.
- Keep paragraphs to 60–75 characters.
- Label every score with **band + coverage + model version + caveat**.
- Label unshipped features as **"planned"**.
- Use icons **plus** labels for mode and severity — never colour alone.
- Prefer whitespace and a single accent over decorative gradients.

## 3. Visual DON'T

- ❌ Pink or rose as the dominant brand hue.
- ❌ Bridal/wedding imagery to mean "matrimony".
- ❌ Swipe-roulette card mechanics.
- ❌ Personality-type badges presented as destiny (INFJ → soulmate).
- ❌ Fake member counts, fake testimonials, fake "active now" pressure.
- ❌ Countdown timers, fake discounts, "only 3 left".
- ❌ Auto-playing carousels; scroll-jacking; parallax on text.
- ❌ E2EE / biometric-verification / "unbreakable privacy" claims that are not
  literally true.
- ❌ Dark "command centre" admin aesthetics or surveillance iconography.
- ❌ Gradients as identity at the expense of contrast.
- ❌ The clay accent (`#D98A3D`) as text colour — use `#8A5A1E`.

## 4. Mode accent usage

| Mode | Accent (light) | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Friendship | `#1F6F5C` teal | chips, badges, section rules |
| Dating | `#A83E50` rose-clay | chips, badges, section rules |
| Matrimony | `#3E4A6B` ink-blue | chips, badges, section rules |

**Never** three different UIs. One product; the accent merely labels context.
Always with icon + text label.

## 5. Component voice (microcopy patterns)

| Element | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Primary CTA | verb-first, concrete | "Start with a few questions" |
| Secondary | plain link | "See how matching works" |
| Save & resume | reassure, no guilt | "We'll remember where you were." |
| Skip | neutral | "Skip for now" (never "No thanks, I don't want to find love") |
| Empty state | honest + next step | "There aren't enough people in your city yet. Here's what we'll do…" |
| Error | what + why + fix | "We couldn't save that. Check your connection and try again." |
| Safety | factual | "Blocking removes them from your discovery and stops contact." |
| Cancel | no friction theatre | "Cancel renewal" → "Renewal stopped. Access continues until 2 Dec." |

## 6. Legal/claims checklist before publishing any copy

- [ ] No guaranteed outcome.
- [ ] No accuracy percentage without a defined ground truth.
- [ ] No compliance claim without counsel sign-off.
- [ ] No verification claim without a running service.
- [ ] No testimonial that isn't real and consented.
- [ ] No statistic that isn't sourced and dated.
