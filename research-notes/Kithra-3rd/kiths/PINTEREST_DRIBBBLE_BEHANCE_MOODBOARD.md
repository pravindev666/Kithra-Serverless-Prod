**Ethics.** No proprietary image, UI asset, or another app's design has been
copied into this project. This file lists **reference URLs and analysis only**.
No third-party image files are redistributed. Where a board is paywalled or
login-gated, that is stated and left as a gap.

**Caveat.** Pinterest/Behance/Dribbble are **visual inspiration, not evidence**
that any palette, layout or typeface improves conversion, trust or retention.

## 2. Analysis — what actually transfers (and what does not)

**Transfers:**
- **Monochrome + one accent** (M1): warmth without pink saturation; gives
  headroom for the mode accents (friendship / dating / matrimony) to mean
  something because the base is quiet.
- **Serif headline + utilitarian sans** (M1, M2): reads as credible/editorial,
  which suits *all three* modes — unlike playful-only or bridal-only treatments.
- **Editorial layout with generous whitespace + one strong human photograph per
  section** (M8): matches the founder's "warm, alive, premium" brief.

**Does NOT transfer:**
- **Pink/rose saturation** (M5, M6, M7): reads as gendered dating; the R/UX
  discussion and the founder's own note both flag it.
- **Swipe-card centralism** (M3, M4): wrong interaction model for deterministic
  compatibility.
- **Illustrated mascots / heavy gradients** (common on Dribbble shots): too
  playful for matrimony intent.

## 3. Photography art-direction hypotheses (to test, not assert)

H1. Natural, unretouched daylight portraits out-perform studio glamour on
    trust (consistent with "too hot to trust", §1 of the lit review).
H2. Group/activity context photos communicate *friendship* intent better than
    single portraits — and are essential so friendship ≠ dating.
H3. A quiet, dignified single portrait (no wedding imagery) communicates
    matrimony intent better than bridal clichés.
H4. Indian + international adult representation should be *interleaved*, not
    segregated into "local" and "global" strips.

## 4. Gaps and limits

- Pinterest boards are dynamic and partially login-gated; captured as URLs.
- Dribbble/Behance shots are *concepts*, frequently never shipped, and their
  colour measurement is unreliable from thumbnails — used only for direction.
- No claim that any referenced design "converts better" is made or implied.
