## A. Terms of Service — draft
**1. Contracting party and service.** These Terms govern use of Kithra operated by `[LEGAL_ENTITY]`, `[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]`, `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`. Kithra provides optional friendship, dating and matrimony discovery, questionnaires, introductions, communities and communications. Kithra does **not** promise a match, friendship, romance, marriage or psychological accuracy. Our index scores are descriptive tools, not clinical assessment or guarantees.

**2. Eligibility.** You must be at least `[AGE_POLICY: initially 18 years]` and legally capable of agreement. You must provide accurate and current personal information, keep credentials private, and not use an account for another individual without permission. Users pursuing matrimony are responsible for any additional marriage-law eligibility and accurate self-disclosure; Kithra's self-report is not official verification.

**3. Separate intent modes.** Friendship, Dating and Matrimony are voluntary and separate. Activation of one does not automatically enroll you in another. You can pause or close a mode, restrict who sees each profile and withdraw optional questionnaire sharing according to the Privacy Notice.

**4. Assessments and model limits.** Questionnaires may be incomplete, culturally imperfect or updated. Scores reflect disclosed model rules and measured responses at the relevant time. They cannot reconstruct a brain or prove the future quality of a relationship. You may decline optional questions and assess match recommendations with independent judgment.

**5. Conduct.** You must not impersonate others, harass, stalk, threaten, cheat, coerce, solicit illegal payments, publish nonconsensual images, target minors, or circumvent blocks or restrictions. In friendship mode respect platonic boundaries; dating/matrimony interactions require mutual intent and respect. Kithra may restrict access, remove content, investigate reports and cooperate with authorities consistent with law and fair procedure.

**6. User content and license.** You retain your rights in your uploaded original content. You provide a nonexclusive, purpose-limited license necessary to host and display content to audiences you select, operate messaging and enforce community safety. This does not authorize selling your private quiz answers or training third-party AI models without separate consent.

**7. Paid features.** Prices and applicable taxes are displayed before checkout. Digital entitlements, renewal dates, cancellation, refunds, errors and disputes are explained in the Billing Policy. Safety, blocking, reports, core data rights and account deletion are never paywalled. Refund rights granted by law cannot be waived through these Terms.

**8. Communication and meeting safety.** Users are strangers and can misrepresent themselves. Exercise judgment, meet initially in public, never transfer money to an unknown person, and use in-product reports for abuse. Kithra does not conduct comprehensive criminal/background checks unless a specific feature truthfully says otherwise.

**9. Suspension, appeals, termination.** We may restrict an account for credible abuse or legal compliance while offering a documented review/appeal path where appropriate. You may delete your account; data handling follows the Privacy Notice and legally necessary retention.

**10. Liability and disputes.** To the extent legally permissible, Kithra does not guarantee outcomes or uninterrupted availability. Nothing here excludes liability, consumer protection rights or remedies that applicable law prohibits limiting. Dispute forum, governing law, contact and required processes: `[COUNSEL_APPROVED_TERMS]`.

**11. Changes.** Notify users of material changes in accessible language; secure new consent when required for a materially new processing purpose. **Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`.
