## 3. Working Hypotheses for Mom Test Pricing Discovery

These figures are **research hypotheses**, not authorized live billing rates:

1. **Friendship Core:** **Always ₹0 Free.**  
   *Justification:* Indian consumers will not pay to find weekend friends or book club companions. Charging for friendship destroys network liquidity immediately.
2. **Dating Exploration:**  
   *Free Tier:* 3–10 daily curated profiles based on reflections.  
   *Premium Experiment:* ₹299–₹499 / month for uncapped profiles and full 12-domain radars.
3. **Matrimony Alignment:**  
   *Free Tier:* Core discovery and mutual similarity.  
   *Premium Experiment:* ₹999 / quarter for Pre-Marriage Alignment Kits and In-Law boundary worksheets.
4. **Cofounder Beta:** **Always ₹0 Free (Invitation Only).**  
   *Justification:* Follows Y Combinator model during research. Founders provide invaluable feedback on working-style rubrics.
5. **The Unified 1-Year Pass:** **₹2,499 / year (₹208 / month).**  
   *Justification:* Unlocks all spaces, all kits, and unlimited discovery for 365 days.
