## 3. What Was Built, What Was Fixed, and What Worked

### 1. The Eight Archetypes: Scientific Demotion & Protection
* **What was broken:** In previous code, if a member answered questions in only one area, the engine averaged primary constructs and assigned an arbitrary score of 50 to unmeasured areas. Answering just 3 questions could produce an unearned, misleading archetype.
* **What was fixed:** Missing constructs now remain strictly `undefined` and are **never assigned 50**. If an archetype has $< 2$ primary constructs measured, the system marks `hasSufficientMultidimensionalCoverage: false`. If $< 15$ questions are answered, archetype assignment is strictly `null`.
* **What worked:** Archetypes are now clearly communicated as optional descriptive summaries of continuous traits, not permanent labels or moral grades.

### 2. Four Separate Matching Models from One Foundation
* **Friendship:** Matches based on platonic interests, social battery, and communication rhythm. Strictly ignores romantic exclusivity and matrimony children gates.
* **Dating:** Enforces intentional dating purpose (`committed_long_term`, `open_to_marriage`) and mutual relationship structure (monogamy vs open).
* **Matrimony:** Enforces marriage timelines, family boundaries, joint financial philosophies, and children goals.
* **Cofounders ("Fire Meets Air"):** Evaluates directional two-way skill coverage (Does Arjun provide the enterprise sales that Neha needs? Does Neha provide the full-stack AI engineering that Arjun needs?). Evaluates funding alignment (Bootstrapped vs VC) and time commitment. Strictly ignores romantic gates.

### 3. The Cross-Context Privacy Firewall (Auto-Suppression)
* If two members connect as cofounders to build a startup, Kithra **automatically hides both members** from each other's romantic Dating and Matrimony feeds.
* Zero cross-context leakage. A member's professional life and private romantic search remain strictly firewalled under the same account.

## 4. What Failed or Was Deliberately Rejected

* **The "General Compatibility Score" for Founders:** We strictly rejected computing a single "92% Cofounder Compatibility" number. Startups are too complex for a single percentage. We replaced it with an explainable 4-pillar report: Shared Direction, Skill Complementarity, Working-Style Compatibility, and Unresolved Questions.
* **Shuffling Likert Rating Options:** We rejected scrambling the visual order of ordered rating scales (e.g. putting "Strongly Disagree" next to "Strongly Agree"). Doing so confuses users and degrades measurement validity.
* **Moral Character & Deception Diagnoses:** We rejected creating "Deception Scores" or labeling users as "Inconsistent". When answers vary across contexts (e.g. festivals vs exhausted Fridays), Kithra honors it as human nuance.

---

## 5. Remaining Weaknesses & Psychometric Limitations

1. **Client Shell Integration for Cofounders:** As documented in our reality audit, the Cofounder Synergy Engine is fully verified in backend domain logic and test suites, but user-facing tabs in `MemberViews.tsx` currently only expose Friendship, Dating, and Matrimony.
2. **Item Calibration ($N = 125$):** Item parameters currently reflect expert-reviewed pilot calibrations rather than multi-thousand human empirical Item Response Theory (IRT) estimations.
3. **Cultural Sub-Demographics:** While questions are localized for Indian cultural contexts (joint families, festivals), full Differential Item Functioning (DIF) across regional languages requires the upcoming $N=300$ pilot cohort.
