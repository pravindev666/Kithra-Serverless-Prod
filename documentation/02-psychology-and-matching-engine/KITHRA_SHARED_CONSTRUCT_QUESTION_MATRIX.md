## 1. The Core Architecture: Shared Nonclinical Psychology

Rather than building 4 completely fragmented apps with repetitive questionnaires, Kithra establishes **one rigorous nonclinical psychological foundation**. 

A person's core communication habits, conflict de-escalation tendencies, personal boundaries, and planning rhythms remain fundamentally consistent across life domains. What changes between spaces is **the context, the vulnerability level, and the specific mutual non-negotiables**.

## 2. Shared-Construct vs. Intention-Specific Question Matrix (125-Item Bank)

The 125-item candidate bank is structured into 3 distinct operational tiers:
1. **Tier 1: Core Shared Items (Items 1–37, 64–75):** Reusable across all 4 spaces with member consent.
2. **Tier 2: Romantic & Domestic Intentional Items (Items 38–63, 76–110):** Specific to Dating and Matrimony; strictly quarantined from Friendship and Cofounders.
3. **Tier 3: Professional Partnership Items (Items 111–125):** Specific to Cofounders; strictly quarantined from Romantic feeds.

## 3. Privacy, Consent & Deduplication Protocol (DPDP Act 2023 Compliance)

To eliminate member fatigue without violating privacy boundaries or Indian DPDP Act 2023 regulations, Kithra enforces the following **Triple-Lock Deduplication Protocol**:

```
                       TRIPLE-LOCK DEDUPLICATION PROTOCOL

    [User Answers Question in Friendship] ──► [Stored in Encrypted Central Response Vault]
                                                                │
                                                                ▼
                     [User Later Enables Dating or Cofounders Space]
                                                                │
                                                                ▼
               ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
               │ Consent Gate Prompt:                                           │
               │ "You answered 5 core communication reflections in Friendship.   │
               │  Would you like to apply these answers to your new space to    │
               │  save time? You can review or edit them anytime."              │
               └───────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                       │
                      ┌────────────────┴────────────────┐
                      ▼                                 ▼
              [Member Accepts]                  [Member Declines]
                      │                                 │
                      ▼                                 ▼
         [Responses Dynamically Reused]    [Fresh Space Assessment Administered]
         [Zero Repetitive Questions]       [Isolated Responses Maintained]
```

### Privacy Firewall Invariants
1. **Intention-Lock Invariant:** Intimate questions answered in Dating (e.g. Q39 physical affection) **can never be queried or rendered** by the Cofounder or Friendship engines.
2. **Global Disconnection Invariant:** If a member edits an answer in a shared construct, derived scores refresh across authorized spaces while maintaining an auditable trace of the update timestamp.
3. **Fail-Closed Consent:** If a member enables a new space but does not explicitly check *"Apply previous reflections"*, the engine defaults to treating those constructs as unmeasured in the new space until answered.
