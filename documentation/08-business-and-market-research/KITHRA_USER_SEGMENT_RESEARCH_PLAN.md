# KITHRA — USER SEGMENT RESEARCH PLAN & RECRUITMENT PROTOCOL
**Framework:** Rigorous Customer Discovery & Qualitative Research  
**Target Geography:** Urban India (Tier 1 & Tier 2: Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, Kochi)  
**Current Audit Status:** `CUSTOMER EVIDENCE: NOT YET COLLECTED`

---

## 1. RESEARCH OBJECTIVE & ETHICAL GUARDRAIL

The goal of this research plan is **not** to find people who will say nice things about Kithra. 

The goal is to study the real, unvarnished behaviors, frustrations, and financial commitments of 25–35 diverse Indian adults seeking companions.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Anti-Confirmation Bias Rule:** Under no circumstances should the founder interview close personal friends, immediate family members, Kithra team members, or investors for customer discovery. These groups will lie to you to protect your feelings.

---

## 2. TARGET SEGMENT MATRIX & SAMPLE SIZES

To achieve theoretical saturation across different relationship intents, the initial research cohort requires **30 participants** divided across 5 distinct segments:

| Segment ID | Segment Focus | Sample Size | Ideal Profile Criteria | Excluded Profile Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SEG-A** | Active Matrimony / Long-Term Seekers | 6–8 Participants | Ages 24–36; actively searching for marriage partner in last 6 months; managing their own search or collaborating with parents. | People whose parents control 100% of the search without their input. |
| **SEG-B** | Regular Dating App Users | 6–8 Participants | Ages 21–32; currently has Tinder, Bumble, or Hinge installed; opened app $\ge 3$ times in last 14 days. | Casual onlookers who haven't matched or met anyone in over a year. |
| **SEG-C** | Platonic Friendship & Community Seekers | 5–6 Participants | Ages 22–38; relocated to a new metro within the past 18 months; actively trying to build social circles. | People who already have a closed, established childhood friend group in their hometown. |
| **SEG-D** | Churned / Disillusioned App Users | 5–6 Participants | Ages 23–35; deleted all dating or matrimony apps in the past 6 months after experiencing burnout. | People who stopped using apps simply because they entered a monogamous marriage. |
| **SEG-E** | Verified Paying Customers | 4–6 Participants | Ages 24–38; has spent $\ge ₹500$ of their own money on dating/matrimony services in the past 12 months. | People on free trials or promotional credits. |

## 4. INFORMED CONSENT & PRIVACY PROTOCOL

Before recording or taking notes, the interviewer must verbally establish:

1. **Voluntary Participation:** The participant can end the conversation at any time or decline to answer any question without losing their honorarium.
2. **Strict Anonymity:** No names, phone numbers, or employer details will ever be published or tied to interview transcripts. Participants are coded as `P-01`, `P-02`, etc.
3. **No Emotional Coercion:** We are studying the *process* of finding partners, not asking for traumatic private relationship details. If a participant feels uncomfortable, immediately pivot: *"No worries at all, let's skip past that."*
4. **Data Retention:** Audio recordings (if consented) are deleted after transcription. Transcripts are stored in access-controlled storage for 90 days, in compliance with Section 8 & Section 27 of the Indian DPDP Act 2023.

## 5. EVIDENCE CODING SCHEME

To prevent the founder from cherry-picking quotes that validate their preconceived notions, all findings must be tagged using this strict coding scheme:

```
[ACTUAL_BEHAVIOR]  ── Past real actions taken ("I deleted Hinge on March 3rd after 4 dry dates").
[MONEY_SPENT]      ── Financial commitment ("I paid ₹1,800 for 3 months of Bumble Boost").
[TIME_INVESTED]    ── Real time committed ("I spend 45 minutes every evening scrolling profiles").
[WORKAROUND_BUILT] ── Homebrewed solution ("My friend and I made an Excel sheet to track matrimonial biodatas").
[HYPOTHETICAL]     ── Unreliable future opinion ("I would totally pay ₹1,000 if it worked").
[POLITE_FLATTERY]  ── Worthless compliments ("That sounds like a really cool startup idea").
```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Golden Filter:** Only `[ACTUAL_BEHAVIOR]`, `[MONEY_SPENT]`, and `[WORKAROUND_BUILT]` count as evidence. `[HYPOTHETICAL]` and `[POLITE_FLATTERY]` must be completely discarded when evaluating product-market fit.

---

## 6. SYNTHESIS & SKEPTICISM SCORECARD

After completing all 30 interviews, the team will answer 4 binary validation questions:

1. **Problem Severity:** Did at least 20 out of 30 participants describe a recent, concrete experience where incompatible values or communication styles cost them significant emotional distress, time, or money? *(Yes / No)*
2. **Active Workarounds:** Are participants currently spending time or money on active workarounds (e.g. paying matchmakers, hiring coaches, creating Notion databases, attending offline mixers)? *(Yes / No)*
3. **Questionnaire Tolerance:** In their actual past behavior, have they ever voluntarily completed more than 5 questions on any app, or did they bounce? *(Yes / No)*
4. **Willingness to Pay:** Have at least 10 out of 30 participants actually spent their own money on a relationship service in the past 12 months? *(Yes / No)*

If the answer to any of these is **No**, Kithra must pivot its strategy before writing another line of code.
