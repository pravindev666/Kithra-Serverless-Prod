## 1. Architectural Overview & Intention Isolation

In accordance with Kithra's founding vision:
> *"Not who society thinks you should love. Who truly understands you."*

The Kithra Connection Assessment (KCA) rejects superficial sorting proxies (caste, appearance, height, salary, or social status). Instead, connection compatibility is operationalized across **14 core psychological and behavioral constructs**. 

Each construct is defined with:
- **Operational Definition:** Rigorous empirical behavioral definition.
- **Facet Breakdown:** Distinct constituent dimensions.
- **Intention Relevance Matrix:** Specific operational behavior in **Friendship**, **Dating**, and **Matrimony**.
- **Measurement Category:** Clear separation of continuous traits, categorical preferences, value trade-offs, and gates.
- **Triangulation Clusters:** Cross-situational scenario anchors.
- **Current Coverage & Gap Analysis:** Current candidate bank items vs planned 240-item target.

## 2. The 14 Priority Relational Constructs

### Construct 1: Emotional Consideration & Affective Attunement
- **Construct ID:** `emotional_consideration` (Layer: `emotional_regulation` / `social_friendship`)
- **Operational Definition:** The extent to which an individual notices, anticipates, and accommodates the emotional states and vulnerabilities of a companion without waiting to be asked.
- **Constituent Facets:**
  1. *Affective Perceptiveness:* Sensitivity to micro-expressions and tonal shifts.
  2. *Emotional Holding:* Ability to offer steady psychological presence during companion distress.
  3. *Unsolicited Warmth:* Spontaneous small gestures communicating care and reassurance.
- **Intention Relevance:**
  - *Friendship:* Validating life updates; checking in during difficult career or family phases.
  - *Dating:* Sensing date fatigue, pacing conversation, ensuring mutual physical and psychological ease.
  - *Matrimony:* Sustained dyadic coregulation over months and years of domestic intimacy.
- **Measurement Classification:** `continuous_trait`.
- **Triangulation Mapping:** Anchored in Cluster 6 (`stress_regulation_and_decompression`) and Cluster 7 (`public_vs_private_affection`).
- **Items & Gap Analysis:** Currently represented by 9 items (e.g., Q14, Q25, Q110). Gap: 7 additional items needed for cross-life stage validation.

### Construct 2: Trust Expectations & Predictability
- **Construct ID:** `trust_expectations` (Layer: `relational_expectations` / `personal_values`)
- **Operational Definition:** The standards and milestones required for an individual to extend emotional vulnerability, financial transparency, and personal reliance to a companion.
- **Constituent Facets:**
  1. *Vulnerability Pace:* Immediate intuitive trust vs gradual, earned incremental trust.
  2. *Confidentiality Thresholds:* Strict containment of shared secrets vs permeable social discussion.
  3. *Behavioral Predictability:* Consistency between spoken promises and daily follow-through.
- **Intention Relevance:**
  - *Friendship:* Respecting private disclosures without gossiping.
  - *Dating:* Comfort sharing personal insecurities and emotional history.
  - *Matrimony:* Complete transparency regarding personal liabilities, health, and family obligations.
- **Measurement Classification:** `continuous_trait` (Pace) + `value_tradeoff` (Confidentiality).
- **Triangulation Mapping:** Anchored in Cluster 1 (`friendship_confidentiality_vs_partner_disclosure`) and Cluster 9 (`trust_repair_pace`).
- **Items & Gap Analysis:** Currently represented by 10 items (Q64, Q65, Q116). Gap: 6 items needed targeting post-breach repair.

### Construct 3: Honesty, Candor & Accountability Attitudes
- **Construct ID:** `honesty_accountability` (Layer: `personal_values`)
- **Operational Definition:** An individual's behavioral philosophy regarding truth-telling, the timing of difficult feedback, and taking personal ownership of mistakes without defensiveness.
- **Constituent Facets:**
  1. *Tactful Candor:* Unvarnished immediate honesty vs tactful, emotionally prepared diplomacy.
  2. *Accountability Ownership:* Immediate self-directed apology vs externalizing or contextualizing blame.
  3. *Public-Private Congruence:* Acting with the same moral principles privately as presented publicly.
- **Intention Relevance:**
  - *Friendship:* Giving honest reality checks with kindness.
  - *Dating:* Clear, early communication about feelings and boundaries without ghosting.
  - *Matrimony:* Open ownership of financial missteps, domestic oversights, and relational lapses.
- **Measurement Classification:** `value_tradeoff` (Tact vs Directness) + `categorical_preference` (Apology style).
- **Triangulation Mapping:** Anchored in Cluster 2 (`tactful_candor_timing`) and Cluster 12 (`public_private_congruence`).
- **Items & Gap Analysis:** 10 items implemented (Q8, Q13, Q101, Q103). Gap: 6 items needed on fiscal candor.

### Construct 4: Conflict Communication & Emotional Repair Rhythms
- **Construct ID:** `conflict_communication_repair` (Layer: `conflict_repair`)
- **Operational Definition:** How an individual navigates disagreements, regulates diffuse physiological arousal, accepts repair attempts, and restores psychological safety after relational ruptures.
- **Constituent Facets:**
  1. *Physiological Soothing Cadence:* Immediate verbal resolution vs structured 20-minute timeout for de-escalation.
  2. *Repair Bid Receptivity:* Soft humor, physical touch, or tonal de-escalation acceptance during tension.
  3. *Post-Conflict Reconnection:* Verbal debriefing vs returning to normal warmth without prolonged re-litigation.
- **Intention Relevance:**
  - *Friendship:* Resolving group misunderstandings without passive aggression.
  - *Dating:* Navigating early ideological disagreements without abrupt termination.
  - *Matrimony:* The primary empirical predictor of marital longevity (Gottman 1999).
- **Measurement Classification:** `continuous_trait` (Timeout need) + `categorical_preference` (Reconnection style).
- **Triangulation Mapping:** Anchored in Cluster 6 (`stress_regulation_and_decompression`) and Cluster 11 (`conflict_repair_bids`).
- **Items & Gap Analysis:** 12 items implemented (Q6, Q102, Q114, Q117). Gap: 4 items needed on non-verbal soothing.

### Construct 5: Reciprocity, Reliability & Dependability
- **Construct ID:** `reciprocity_dependability` (Layer: `social_friendship` / `relational_expectations`)
- **Operational Definition:** The balance between giving and receiving support, fidelity to commitments, and punctuality in daily relational life.
- **Constituent Facets:**
  1. *Commitment Follow-Through:* Keeping scheduled plans without frequent casual cancellations.
  2. *Support Reciprocity:* Mutual balance in emotional labor, listening time, and practical favors.
  3. *Crisis Availability:* Readiness to show up physically or logistically when a companion faces emergencies.
- **Intention Relevance:**
  - *Friendship:* Equal effort in initiating meetups and offering support during moves or crises.
  - *Dating:* Reliable attendance, punctuality, and honoring agreed schedules.
  - *Matrimony:* Equitable co-responsibility in domestic duties, childcare, and elderly parent care.
- **Measurement Classification:** `continuous_trait`.
- **Triangulation Mapping:** Anchored in Cluster 8 (`reliability_under_competing_priorities`).
- **Items & Gap Analysis:** 8 items implemented (Q26, Q33, Q122). Gap: 8 items needed on long-term mutual care balance.

### Construct 6: Relationship Commitment & Structural Expectations
- **Construct ID:** `commitment_expectations` (Layer: `relational_expectations`)
- **Operational Definition:** The agreed rules, structural milestones, and exclusivity boundaries governing a romantic or matrimonial partnership.
- **Constituent Facets:**
  1. *Relationship Structure:* Strict monogamy vs consensual non-monogamy / polyamory.
  2. *Commitment Horizon:* Fluid emergent dating vs clear intentional dating toward cohabitation or marriage.
  3. *Relational Exclusivity Expectations:* Emotional, digital, and physical exclusivity boundaries.
- **Intention Relevance:**
  - *Friendship:* Not applicable (intention isolation strictly enforced).
  - *Dating:* Defining whether a connection is casual exploration or monogamous partnership.
  - *Matrimony:* Non-negotiable structural foundation of the legal/familial union.
- **Measurement Classification:** `essential_gate` (Monogamy/Polyamory) + `continuous_trait` (Pacing).
- **Triangulation Mapping:** Gate evaluated authoritatively prior to similarity scoring.
- **Items & Gap Analysis:** 4 items implemented (Q38, Q50, Q106). Gap: 2 items needed on international relocation boundaries.

### Construct 7: Personal Independence, Autonomy & Differentiation
- **Construct ID:** `personal_independence` (Layer: `relational_boundaries`)
- **Operational Definition:** The degree to which an individual maintains separate friendships, personal hobbies, creative projects, and emotional differentiation while deeply bonded to a companion.
- **Constituent Facets:**
  1. *Spatial Autonomy:* Need for a dedicated physical room, solo travel, or independent downtime.
  2. *Social Differentiation:* Maintaining personal friend networks distinct from couple friendships.
  3. *Cognitive Self-Direction:* Ability to hold personal convictions without requiring total companion validation.
- **Intention Relevance:**
  - *Friendship:* Celebrating independent growth without jealousy or possessiveness.
  - *Dating:* Honoring private time and professional obligations without feeling rejected.
  - *Matrimony:* Preventing enmeshment and emotional suffocation in shared domestic living.
- **Measurement Classification:** `continuous_trait`.
- **Triangulation Mapping:** Anchored in Cluster 5 (`family_vs_couple_autonomy`) and Cluster 10 (`solo_solitude_vs_shared_leisure`).
- **Items & Gap Analysis:** 10 items implemented (Q9, Q118, Q120). Gap: 5 items needed on creative solitude.

### Construct 8: Family Boundaries & In-Law Involvement
- **Construct ID:** `family_boundaries` (Layer: `family_domestic` / `relational_boundaries`)
- **Operational Definition:** How an individual negotiates the boundary between the primary couple/companion unit and the influence, opinions, and daily presence of extended family or in-laws.
- **Constituent Facets:**
  1. *Couple Primacy:* Solving major decisions strictly within the couple before informing extended family.
  2. *Joint Living Comfort:* Willingness to co-reside with parents/in-laws vs requirement for independent living.
  3. *Elder Input Weight:* Degree of deference given to elder advice on careers, child-rearing, and lifestyle.
- **Intention Relevance:**
  - *Friendship:* Minimal (friendship boundary independence).
  - *Dating:* Moderate (timing of introducing partner to parents).
  - *Matrimony:* Critical (one of the primary sources of marital divorce in urban South Asia).
- **Measurement Classification:** `value_tradeoff` + `categorical_preference`.
- **Triangulation Mapping:** Anchored in Cluster 5 (`family_vs_couple_autonomy`).
- **Items & Gap Analysis:** 8 items implemented (Q17, Q68, Q69). Gap: 6 items needed on financial elder support protocols.

### Construct 9: Shared Decision-Making & Power Equity
- **Construct ID:** `decision_making_equity` (Layer: `relational_boundaries` / `finances_independence`)
- **Operational Definition:** The process through which two adults resolve competing preferences, allocate resources, and make joint commitments with equal voice and procedural fairness.
- **Constituent Facets:**
  1. *Consensus Seeking:* Striving for mutual enthusiastic buy-in vs taking turns as lead decision-maker.
  2. *Expenditure Thresholds:* The monetary amount that triggers mandatory mutual consultation.
  3. *Relocation & Career Compromise:* Egalitarian balancing of both partners' professional trajectories.
- **Intention Relevance:**
  - *Friendship:* Shared planning for group trips and shared dinner tabs.
  - *Dating:* Choosing dates and activities democratically.
  - *Matrimony:* High-stakes joint decisions (home buying, city relocation, elder care).
- **Measurement Classification:** `continuous_trait` + `value_tradeoff`.
- **Triangulation Mapping:** Anchored in Cluster 4 (`career_relocation_compromise`).
- **Items & Gap Analysis:** 7 items implemented (Q9, Q12, Q121, Q123). Gap: 6 items needed on micro-decision friction.

- **Construct ID:** `lifestyle_routines` (Layer: `daily_lifestyle`)
- **Operational Definition:** Day-to-day behavioral compatibility in circadian sleep schedules, cleanliness thresholds, dietary preferences, and domestic micro-rituals.
- **Constituent Facets:**
  1. *Chronotype Synchronization:* Morning lark vs night owl sleep-wake alignment.
  2. *Orderliness & Cleanliness:* High structure and immediate tidying vs relaxed clutter tolerance.

- **Intention Relevance:**

  - *Dating:* Weekend sleepover ease and dinner timing compatibility.
  - *Matrimony:* Direct daily friction point in cohabitation and mental load distribution.
- **Measurement Classification:** `disclosed_behavior` (Chronotype) + `categorical_preference` (Orderliness).
- **Triangulation Mapping:** Anchored in Cluster 10 (`daily_micro_rituals`).

### Construct 11: Social Energy, Solitude & Community Rhythms
- **Construct ID:** `social_energy_solitude` (Layer: `social_energy`)

- **Construct ID:** `future_expectations` (Layer: `long_term_goals`)

- **Constituent Facets:**
  1. *Temporal Horizon:* 10-year disciplined milestone planning vs spontaneous emergent living.
  2. *Geographic Rootedness:* Anchoring in one hometown vs global mobility and nomadism.
  3. *Capital Accumulation vs Lifestyle Spending:* Early asset freedom vs experiential living.
- **Intention Relevance:**
  - *Friendship:* Shared long-term creative projects or enduring geographic ties.

  - *Matrimony:* Foundational alignment required to prevent bitter mid-life divergences.
