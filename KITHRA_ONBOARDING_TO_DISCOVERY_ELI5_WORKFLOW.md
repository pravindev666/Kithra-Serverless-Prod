## 1. The Core Philosophy: "Profile as You Live"

Traditional dating and matrimonial apps force you to sit through an exhausting 45-minute interrogation before letting you see a single person. Users get tired, start clicking randomly, and end up with terrible matches.

**Kithra works like Naukri or LinkedIn:**
* You can get started and see initial matches in **under 3 minutes** by answering just **5 calm starter questions**.
* You only answer what matters to the specific space you want to explore today.
* You can come back and add reflections whenever you feel like it.
* You **never have to answer all 125 questions** to use Kithra.

### Step 1: Sign Up & The Safety Gate
* You create your account with your email and password.
* You confirm you are **at least 18 years old**. (If you skip this or aren't 18, Kithra strictly locks the door—no exceptions).
* You sign the **Community Respect Pledge** (promising honest intentions, zero sexual harassment, and gracious acceptance of rejection).

### Step 2: Choose Your Primary Space
* You pick which room you want to enter first:
  * 🤝 **Friendship:** Looking for platonic friends, weekend activity buddies, or casual hangouts.
  * 💖 **Dating:** Looking for an intentional, meaningful romantic relationship.
  * 💍 **Matrimony:** Ready for marriage, family alignment, and lifelong partnership.
  * 🚀 **Cofounders:** Looking for a business partner to build a startup with.
* *Note:* You can turn on other spaces later from your Settings, and they will stay strictly separated.

### Step 3: Five Calm Starter Questions (Under 2 Minutes)
Everyone starts with the same 5 non-threatening questions:
1. *Pacing:* Do you like to chat for a few weeks before meeting, or do you prefer meeting for a coffee soon?
2. *Reply Rhythm:* During a busy workweek, do you reply throughout the day or catch up in the evenings?
3. *First Meetup:* Do you prefer a casual tea stall or a quiet walk in a park?
4. *Conversation:* Are you more of a storyteller or a curious listener?
5. *Format:* Do you prefer texting or sending voice notes?

### Step 4: Quick Space-Specific Essentials
Depending on what space you picked, you answer 1 or 2 essential setup questions:
* **In Dating:** What are you looking for? (*Committed long-term*, *Open to marriage*, or *Intentional dating*). Are you looking for monogamy?
* **In Matrimony:** When are you hoping to marry? Do you want children?
* **In Cofounders:** What skills do you bring? (*Engineering*, *Sales*, *Design*, etc.) What skills are you looking for in a partner?
* **In Friendship:** What are your favorite weekend activities?

### Step 5: Your First Provisional Reflection Note
Right after answering your first reflections, Kithra unlocks your **Early Reflection Card**:
> *"Based on your first reflections, you value unhurried, thoughtful communication and prefer quiet, low-pressure conversations."*
* **What Kithra never does here:** We never give you a fake label like *"You are an ENFP"* or *"You are a Mastermind"*. Five questions are enough to know how you like to text—not who your soulmate is.

### Step 6: Discovery & The Intention-Locked Introduction
Now you enter the discovery feed for your space!
1. **You see real profiles:** Each card highlights shared passions, complementary strengths, and clear explanations of why you might get along.
2. **You send an Introduction:** You can't just send a generic "Hey" or a creepy comment. You send a thoughtful, private introduction note.
3. **Mutual Consent:** The other person reviews your profile and decides whether to accept.
4. **Intention-Locked Chat:** Once accepted, you enter a private, 1-on-1 conversation that stays strictly inside that space. If you met in Cofounders, you will never see each other's dating profiles.

## 3. The Naukri-Style Modular Growth (Add Depth Later)

Whenever you log in, your dashboard shows an optional **Self-Discovery Roadmap**:
* 🌿 *Everyday Rhythms (3 mins · 5 questions)*
* 🕊️ *Conflict & Repair (4 mins · 5 questions)*
* 🏡 *Family & Independence (4 mins · 5 questions)*
* 💡 *Deep Values & Aspirations (5 mins · 5 questions)*

You can take one module while waiting for the metro, take another on Sunday morning, or stop whenever you want. Your matches will automatically get smarter and more nuanced with every module you explore.
