## 2. Before vs. After Blueprints for the Five Most Important Screens

### Screen 1: Member Discover (`/member/discover`)
- **BEFORE (Current App):**
  - Flat 3-column grid of tall cards. Each card displays full paragraphs for `Echo`, `Counterpoint`, and `Mosaic Fit` inline. No Personality Group folders. No way to browse by personality type or compare two people side-by-side.
- **AFTER (Redesigned):**
  - **Left Folder Rail (Desktop) / Top Category Bubbles (Mobile):** One-tap browsing across **`All Compatible`**, the **8 Personality Pattern Groups** (`⚓ Steady Anchors`, `🌉 Warm Bridges`, `✦ Curious Sparks`, `❖ Mosaic Builders`, etc.), **Compatibility Lenses** (`Shared Ground`, `Healthy Balance`, `Teamwork Fit`), and **`My Shortlists`**.
  - **Clean 3-Second Cards:** `4:5` portrait (or Constellation Monogram), Name/City, **Personality Group Pill**, **1 warm sentence** explaining why you fit, `Compare` checkbox, and `View Compatibility Story`.

### Screen 2: Answering Questions & Self-Discovery (`/member/self` & `/member/assessments`)
- **BEFORE (Current App):**
  - Shows a `100-Question Theme Map (20 Qs per theme)` with static question cards and metadata about `Signal Effect`, without a personal personality story or auto-advancing card flow.
- **AFTER (Redesigned):**
  - **Unified `My Pattern` Hub (`/member/self`)** with 3 horizontal pill tabs:
    1. **`My Personality Story`:** Celebrates your **Primary Constellation Group** (`⚓ The Steady Anchor`), your **Secondary Undertone** (`🌉 The Warm Bridge`), your top 3 relationship strengths, communication/repair style, and which Personality Groups balance you best.
    2. **`60-Second Question Decks`:** 12 relatable life chapters -> Click any deck to launch a **5-Question Sprint** (1 centered card at a time, tap `1–4` to **auto-advance in `250ms`**, instant insight reward after 5 cards).
    3. **`Private Reflection Journal`:** Calm private notebook restored from `Kithra-main-OLD-CONCEPT`.

### Screen 3: Match Compatibility Story (`/member/match/:id`)
- **BEFORE (Current App):**
  - Shows qualitative signal cards and a technical `Measured Alignment Details Sheet` (`80–90% overlap band`), without Personality Group pairing, without the 4 Everyday Scenarios, and without a Matrimony Family Summary option.
- **AFTER (Redesigned):**
  - **Headline Pairing Banner:** *`⚓ Steady Anchor (You) × 🌉 Warm Bridge (Rohan) — Strong Teamwork Fit`*.
  - **2-Box Story:** *Where You Naturally Click (`Shared Ground`)* + *How You Balance Each Other & What to Keep in Mind (`Teamwork & Balance`)*.
  - **4 Interactive Everyday Scenario Cards:** *Saturday plans change*, *Repairing a tense moment*, *Managing shared expenses*, *Family & personal boundaries*.
  - **Action Bar:** `Send Introduction Note` (with 3 trait icebreakers), `Compare`, `Share Family-Safe Summary` (in Matrimony mode), and `See full theme-by-theme alignment` (collapsed by default).

### Screen 4: Member Home (`/member/home`)
- **BEFORE (Current App):**
  - Greeting, intention selector, 2 profile cards, recent chats, circles, and static secondary cards for assessment/profile completion.
- **AFTER (Redesigned):**
  - Adds your **Personality Constellation Pill** next to your greeting, brings back `Kithra-main-OLD-CONCEPT`'s **"Today's 60-Second Reflection"** inline interactive card right at the top, and displays **Personality Group badges** on spotlight matches.

### Screen 5: Messaging (`/member/messages`)
- **BEFORE (Current App):**
  - Single thread list + message pane.
- **AFTER (Redesigned):**
  - Adds **`Active Chats` vs. `Intro Requests`** filter tabs (so you inspect a sender's Personality Group and Compatibility Story before accepting their chat), **3 Clickable Trait Icebreaker Chips**, and a collapsible **Right-Hand Match Cheat-Sheet**.
